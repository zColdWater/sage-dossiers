Still holding old AERO, VELO, a veNFT or Aerodrome LP?

Aero replaced Aerodrome and Velodrome on 22 October 2026. If you have not upgraded yet, this page lists what to do, in order, with a link to the official source for every fact. It is free and loads nothing from other sites.

Five facts that matter

  1. Nothing converts automatically. Tokens, locks and liquidity all need action in the official upgrade portal.
  2. Legacy emissions have stopped. All Aerodrome and Velodrome gauges were deactivated at launch, so staked legacy LP earns no new emissions.
  3. There is no set deadline. But the official hub says legacy front ends may be shut down later. Anyone telling you to upgrade "today or lose it" is not quoting the official page.
  4. It is one-way. Once you upgrade, you cannot convert back.
  5. The rates are fixed. Legacy AERO converts 1:1 to the new AERO. VELO converts at 0.044 AERO per VELO (calculated from projected supplies, per the hub).

Source: https://aero.xyz/aero-upgrade/

Your steps, in order

What do you hold?

Tick everything that applies and the list below keeps only your steps. Leave everything unticked to see all of them.

  1. Use only the official sites, typed by hand.Upgrade portals are on aerodrome.finance and velodrome.finance; the official guides are aerodrome.finance/docs/upgrade and velodrome.finance/docs/upgrade, and the new app is app.aero.xyz. Do not follow upgrade links from direct messages, replies or ads. (official upgrade hub)
  2. On an exchange? Follow the exchange.Centralized exchanges run their own upgrade process and timeline. Check your exchange's notice first. (official upgrade hub)
  3. Keep some ETH for fees.You need ETH on Base for Aerodrome-side upgrades and ETH on OP Mainnet for Velodrome-side upgrades. A veVELO upgrade also charges a cross-chain messaging fee in ETH. (official upgrade hub)
  4. Multisig or contract wallet: confirm the destination works on Base.The upgraded assets settle on Base. If the receiving address is a contract wallet or multisig, make sure it exists and is usable on Base: transfers to it cannot be reversed. (official upgrade hub)
  5. Bring the veNFT back to your own wallet.Withdraw it from the Relay, vault or protocol first. The upgrade flow only works on a veNFT you hold directly. (official upgrade hub)
  6. Claim every reward before you upgrade.Unclaimed rewards on a veNFT cannot be recovered after the upgrade. The upgrade flow walks you through claiming; do not skip it. (official upgrade hub)
  7. Reset your votes.A veNFT with active votes cannot be upgraded. The upgrade flow includes the reset as a step. (official upgrade hub)
  8. Move VELO to OP Mainnet first.The upgrade flow only picks up VELO that is already on OP Mainnet. Any VELO or xVELO on another chain has to be bridged back first. The Velodrome upgrade starts on OP Mainnet and settles on Base. (official upgrade hub)
  9. Upgrade the lock to sAERO.Your old veNFT ID is retired and you receive a new sAERO position. The remaining lock time carries over, rounded up to the next full week; a permanent lock becomes sAERO at Maximum Stake Duration. (official upgrade hub)
  10. Upgrade liquid tokens.Use the upgrade portal: AERO converts 1:1, VELO converts at 0.044 AERO. (official upgrade hub)
  11. Withdraw ALM positions by hand.Deposits managed by an ALM do not move automatically. Withdraw them and manage the liquidity yourself. (official upgrade hub)
  12. Pools paired with VELO: withdraw, then upgrade the VELO.Withdraw the position, upgrade the VELO, and then provide liquidity again in an upgraded pool. (official upgrade hub)
  13. Move liquidity where a matching pool exists; otherwise withdraw.Not every legacy pool has a matching Aero pool. Where the upgrade flow offers a 1:1 destination, use it; otherwise withdraw manually and, if you want, create a new pool on the Liquidity tab. Liquidity in eligible Aero pools earns AERO rewards. (official upgrade hub)
  14. Before you sign: check the contract address.Compare any contract you are asked to approve or call with the official security page. The checker below covers the main migration and core contracts. (official security page)

Red flags (Sage's own advice, not from the official page)

  • A "migration" or "claim" link that reached you by direct message, reply or ad.
  • A countdown or "final deadline". The official hub says there is no set deadline.
  • Any request for your seed phrase or private key. No upgrade ever needs it.
  • A request to send tokens to a plain wallet address instead of using the portal.
  • An approval for a contract that is not on the official security page, or that only looks like one.
Who controls Aero now? The security page lists about 15 access roles and an Emergency Council multisig that can switch gauges off and pause the migration contracts, but it publishes no multisig addresses, signers or thresholds. Read the full sourced dossier: Aero control dossier
