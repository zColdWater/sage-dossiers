Facts
Aero went live on 21 Oct 2026 at 8:00 PM EDT (22 Oct 00:00 UTC), when Aerodrome and Velodrome upgraded together. primary-verified Sources: aero-hub
Emissions on Aerodrome and Velodrome stopped at launch and all gauges are deactivated. Both front ends stay live to support upgrades; front ends for legacy protocols may be shut down at a later date. There is no set upgrade deadline. primary-verified Sources: aero-hub
Nothing converts automatically for self-custody holders, and the upgrade cannot be reversed. AERO converts 1:1; VELO converts at 0.044 AERO. primary-verified Sources: aero-hub
The Block reported on 2026-10-21 that the merged protocol, Aero, runs across seven networks (Ethereum mainnet, Base, Robinhood Chain, Ink, OP Mainnet, Arc, Arbitrum). secondary-reported Sources: block-aero-merge
What it means for you
If you still LP, lock or vote on legacy Aerodrome, you are earning nothing new there. Claim rewards, reset votes and upgrade through the official portal (see the Aero upgrade checklist), or withdraw.

中文摘要

Aerodrome 已被 Aero 取代（2026-10-22 00:00 UTC 上线）。旧版排放在 Aero 上线时就停了，所有旧 gauge 都已停用，质押在旧池子里的 LP 不会再拿到新排放。旧前端现在只为升级保留，之后可能关闭。升级没有截止日，但不会自动完成，而且不可逆。权限层面：团队可以暂停 v2 池的兑换（加减流动性不受影响），也可以设置手续费；紧急委员会可以关停或恢复 gauge。文档列出的角色里，没有一个能直接提走 LP 本金。
