Governance Attack Cost (GAC) calculator

The dollar cost of buying enough votes to pass a proposal on your own, before slippage, compared with the money that proposal could move.

GAC = max(quorum_votes, opposing_votes + 1) × token_price
leverage = value_at_risk / GAC

Turn on JavaScript to use the calculator, or apply the formula above by hand.

How to read it

  • Red: GAC below 1% of the value at risk. Only guardians, vetoers and timelocks stand in the way.
  • Amber: 1% to 10%.
  • Green: 10% or more.

GAC is a lower bound. Slippage raises the real cost; borrowing or renting votes lowers it. A long voting delay, a timelock and an active guardian all reduce real-world risk even when GAC is low. The March 2026 Moonwell Moonriver case is the example to remember: about $1,800 of MFAM was reported to put about $1.08M at risk, roughly 600× leverage.
