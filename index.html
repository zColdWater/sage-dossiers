Dossiers

Aero (merged Aerodrome + Velodrome), Base hub

elevated (opacity: key holders unpublished) as of 2026-11-21 · 6 open unknowns

Aero is one month old. Control is split across about 15 named access roles plus an Emergency Council multisig that can switch gauges off and pause the migration contracts. The official security page publishes no multisig addresses, signers or thresholds, so you cannot yet check who holds those keys. The contracts I checked on block explorers are source-verified and share one deployer, and basescan shows no proxy for the AERO token.

Morpho Blue (Morpho Markets V1) — core contract

low (core only) as of 2026-11-20 · 4 open unknowns

Core contract has no upgrade path; the owner can only add options and take up to 25% of interest. Your risk lives in which market/vault you pick, not in the core.

Moonwell (Base deployment)

elevated (governance + incidents) as of 2026-11-20 · 4 open unknowns

Token-holder governance with a token-denominated quorum and a 1-minute voting delay. Its sister deployment on Moonriver was hit by a ~$1,800 governance takeover attempt in March 2026 that put ~$1.08M at risk, and Moonwell took ~$1.8M of bad debt from an oracle misconfiguration earlier in 2026.

Aerodrome Finance (legacy v2 contracts), superseded by Aero

high (lifecycle: deprecated) as of 2026-11-21 · 3 open unknowns

Deprecated: Aero replaced Aerodrome on 22 Oct 2026 (00:00 UTC). Legacy emissions stopped at launch and every legacy gauge is deactivated, so staked legacy LP earns nothing new. The legacy front end stays up only to support upgrades and may be shut down later. Admin roles could pause swaps, set fees and kill gauges, but no documented role can withdraw LP principal.

Why this exists

Bytecode scanners tell you whether a contract has an owner. They don't tell you who the owner is, what it is not allowed to do, how cheaply the governance could be bought, or whether the protocol is being wound down. Those answers live in docs, governance forums and explorers. Collecting them takes hours, so most people skip it. These dossiers do that work once and publish it for everyone.
